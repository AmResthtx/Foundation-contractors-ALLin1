# Helical Pier Project Examples — Structural Documentation Standard

Living reference per POLICIES.md Policy 1 (positioning) and Policy 3 (exact alloy/spec
tracking). Ellis-provided project sets (2026-07-02) establishing what a complete,
professional helical pier structural package looks like — the bar for our own
documentation, proposals, and (with permission per Policy "Use of Local Projects in
Messaging") case-study content. Trace IDs refer to `sources.md` § Project references
(internal only — not Policy-1-approved public sources; do not cite externally without
project-specific permission, per backlog R-4).

## PRJ-002 — Ward-French Residence (Bellville, TX) — reference standard

Full custom-residence structural set, INSIGHT Structures Inc., project 23-1116.171,
"Preliminary Pricing" issue 03/08/2024. **This is the clearest example we have of a
complete helical pile design package** and should be the template for how we spec and
document our own installs. Trace: PRJ-002.

### Design criteria (Sheet S1.1, General Notes – Foundation, items 8–18)
- Helical piles designed to resist a **total load of 50 kips working stress, minimum
  factor of safety of 2**.
- Design and manufacture must comply with the current **IBC** as adopted by the local
  jurisdiction, and with the geotechnical report's recommendations.
- Pile system must be **ICC-ES recognized** with a current ESR showing compliance with
  **AC358** and the current IBC (matches SRC-001/SRC-004).
- Central shaft: **round only** — no other shaft shapes permitted in this spec.
- Helix plate count, size, spacing, and thickness sized to the specified design load
  (manufacturer-engineered per pile, not a fixed catalog value).
- Installation torque: minimum allowable torque must be high enough to achieve
  required bearing capacity **including the safety factor of 2**; maximum allowable
  torsional capacity of the shaft must never be exceeded.
- Pile shafts must be designed/manufactured to resist all stresses induced by
  installation itself (not just service loads).
- Installer must confirm existing conditions/underground obstructions (probing or
  scanning) and report any unforeseen obstruction to the structural engineer — pile
  locations cannot be changed without the engineer's written approval.

### Torque-log / special-inspection record — directly answers backlog R-9
Item 17 specifies the **exact field list** a special inspector must record, sign, and
seal (by a registered PE), for every helical pile installation:
1. Installation date
2. Pile manufacturer
3. Installation contractor
4. Identification of installation equipment
5. Minimum allowable installation torque
6. Maximum allowable installation torque
7. Central shaft diameter of each pile
8. Helix plate configuration of each pile
9. Actual tip embedment of each pile
10. Actual installation torque of each pile
11. Ultimate capacity of each pile, as specified by manufacturer
12. Allowable capacity of each pile, as specified by manufacturer

**This is the field list Hermes should require in the payment-blocking torque-log
verification (Policy 6, backlog R-9)** — it's a real engineer's minimum bar for a
sealed inspection report, not a hypothetical.

### Foundation schedule (Sheet S1.3, "AA — Standard Helical Pile Details")
| Grade beam | Width | Depth | Min embedment | Reinforcing | Stirrups |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Interior | 12" | 30" | n/a | 2-#6 top, 2-#4 mid, 2-#6 bottom | #3 @ 12" oc |
| Exterior | 12" | 30" | 18" | 2-#6 top, 2-#4 mid, 2-#6 bottom | #3 @ 12" oc |

- Slab: 6" typ., #4 @ 12" oc each way at mid-depth.
- Grade beam cage held 3" clear from earth-formed faces; beam width varies where noted
  in plan.
- Two pile types detailed: **standard battered pile** and **standard vertical pile**
  (battered used at perimeter/skewed conditions per plan).
- Pile design calculations and fabrication drawings must be signed/sealed by a PE and
  reviewed by the structural EOR **before** fabrication and installation.

## PRJ-001 — Lumberton MUD, Matthews Manhole Replacement (Hardin County, TX)

LJA Engineering, TCEQ Bond Series 2022A, sheet S7 "Structural Details", approved
03/07/2024. Helical piles here support a municipal sanitary-sewer manhole structure,
not a residential foundation — useful for load-capacity comparison, not as a
residential template. Trace: PRJ-001.

- **4 helical piles at 5' × 5' spacing** plus **1 helical pile centered under the pile
  cap** (5 piles total).
- Pile loading: **40 kips compression per pile**, lateral load negligible.
- Preliminary embedment estimate: **30' into earth** (pile top elevation determined in
  field per manhole construction method).
- Reinforced concrete pile cap: 8'-0" sq., Class HES (High Early Strength) / Type II,
  (12) #6 bent bars each-way top and bottom, tied into a cage designed to allow drop
  into excavation rather than tying in-excavation.
- Site condition: geotech report showed water table ~13' below grade at time of
  report (varies seasonally) — contractor should anticipate dewatering the excavation.
- Piles installed from top of excavation per general contractor note; helical pile
  contractor confirmed feasibility directly on the drawing.
- Reference geotechnical report: **No. B2212127** (not in our possession — cite only
  if we obtain it).

## Non-example: pool foundation (drilled piers, not helical)

**PRJ-003 — 12906 Hermitage Lane, Houston (Texas Pools / Dunaway|BEC)**, "For Review"
issue 02/09/2024. This project uses **9/36" drilled concrete piers to 12' below
grade**, not helical piles — a different foundation system entirely (bored/drilled
pier vs. helical screw pile). Kept here only as a negative example: if a future
project reference gets pulled into our content pipeline, verify it's actually a
helical pile job before using it as a helical-pier case study. Trace: PRJ-003.
